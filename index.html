The Challenge

The Nuzlocke begins after Ann's awakening. Played on Hard. Catch one Persona per area, keep your party under the boss-level cap, and never use a fallen teammate again. Win by reaching the true ending with at least one original Phantom Thief still standing.

Core Rules
  • Permadeath. If a party member (non-MC) falls in battle, they are dead — remove them from the active party and never use them again.
  • MC death = Game Over. If Joker goes down, the run ends.
  • One Persona per area. A single Persona per Palace / Mementos area. No fusion-catching, no Compendium summons, no DLC Personas.
  • Fusing is allowed — but only from Personas you legally obtained. Request rewards don't count toward the per-area limit.
  • Starter exception. Getting Pixie at the start doesn't count. Fusing Arsène + Pixie doesn't count — re-summon them via the Compendium.
Combat
  • Hard difficulty only. No dropping down mid-run.
  • Party level ≤ boss level. Your highest party level must stay at or below the cap of the boss you're heading toward (see the cap table).
Victory
  • Reach the true ending.
  • Have at least one original party member alive at the end.
